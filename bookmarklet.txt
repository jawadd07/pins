HOW TO USE (one time setup, 1 minute)
1. Chrome: Ctrl+Shift+O (bookmarks manager) > right click > Add new bookmark.
   Name: Grab Picks
   URL: paste the ONE long line below (starts with javascript:)
2. Open amazon.com, search a niche, e.g.  boho decor,  sort by Avg. Customer Review.
3. Click bookmark "Grab Picks" > type category (boho / home-decor / kitchen / slow-living) > OK.
4. It copies up to 10 well-reviewed products (4.2 stars+, 300 reviews+, no ads). Paste into picks.txt. Save.
5. Double-click auto.bat. Done.

Needs Amazon page layout unchanged. Not tested on live Amazon. If it says no products or errors, tell Claude.
Reads only the page you are looking at, one click per page. Do not run it in loops.
